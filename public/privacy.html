Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026 • WEB STUDIO 46, founded by Sangiv VP

1. Introduction & Ownership

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which WEB STUDIO 46 (“the Studio”, “we”, “our”, or “us”), founded and directed by Sangiv VP based out of Gobichettipalayam, Erode District, Tamil Nadu, India, collects, utilizes, manages, and safeguards personal data obtained from visitors and prospective clients of our website (webstudio46.com) and communication channels.

We are strictly committed to digital transparency, ethical software craft, and compliance with applicable data protection legislation, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary for project discovery, technical quotation, and professional client engagement. We gather data via the following mechanisms:

  • Direct Client Inquiries: When submitting our Multi-Step Project Enquiry Wizard or contacting us directly via email or WhatsApp, we collect your Full Name, Email Address, Contact Number, Organization/Brand Name, Project Scope, Budget Tier, and Desired Delivery Timeline.
  • Project Assets & Media: Content, logos, brand guides, or reference website links provided voluntarily by you during the consultation and design phase.
  • Technical Metadata & Logs: For security and spam mitigation, our server infrastructure temporarily logs standard technical request metadata (such as IP address, browser user-agent, and submission timestamp) strictly to enforce rate limiting and prevent denial-of-service abuse.

3. Lawful Purpose & Usage of Data

Your personal data is treated with utmost confidentiality and is utilized solely for legitimate business engagements:

  • Reviewing project specifications, feasibility assessments, and delivering formal quotations.
  • Transmitting transactional email confirmations, status updates, and reference ID tracking.
  • Direct architectural consultation, milestone progress reviews, and delivery handover.
  • Generating professional tax invoices and managing post-launch warranty support.

Zero Data Selling Guarantee: We do not sell, rent, monetize, broker, or trade your personal information or project details to any third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing consortiums.

4. Technical Security & Infrastructure Safeguards

WEB STUDIO 46 implements rigorous modern security engineering standards to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Input Sanitization: Strict server-side encoding and stripping of HTML/script injection vectors to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS).
  • Rate Limiting: Automated IP-based request throttling to prevent brute-force attacks and automated form abuse.
  • Hardened HTTP Headers: Active security policies including X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN, and Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin.
  • Isolated Local Persistence: Enquiry records are stored securely with atomic read/write locks, preventing accidental database corruption.

5. Third-Party Integrations & Sub-processors

Depending on the services commissioned, project development may involve integration with reputable enterprise service providers:

  • Transaction Messaging: Automated notification emails are routed via established SMTP gateways (such as Google Workspace or Nodemailer relays).
  • Client Third-Party Gateways: For client e-commerce stores, payment gateways (Razorpay, Cashfree, Stripe) and logistics APIs (Shiprocket, Delhivery) are configured directly under the client's verified merchant credentials, ensuring financial data never passes through studio servers.

6. Data Retention & Erasure Rights

We retain project enquiry records only for as long as necessary to fulfill project requirements, provide ongoing support, and maintain lawful financial accounting records. You possess the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request a full copy of the enquiry records and communications archived for your account.
  • Right to Rectification: Request the correction of any inaccurate or outdated contact information.
  • Right to Erasure (Forget): Request the permanent deletion of your enquiry data from our active storage archives at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email our founder directly at webstudio46.in@gmail.com. Requests are acknowledged and processed within 48 business hours.
