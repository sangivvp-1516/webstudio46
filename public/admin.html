How to Access & Use the Excel Sheet (.xlsx)

All enquiry submissions (Name, Phone, Email, Business, Requirements, Budget, Timeline) are automatically written directly into an Excel file on your computer without any manual work.

1
Local File on your Computer:
The file is permanently saved at: Studio_46\data\enquiries.xlsx.
You can simply double-click it in Windows Explorer to open directly in Microsoft Excel, WPS Office, or drag & drop to Google Drive / Sheets.
2
1-Click Download via Browser:
Click the green “Download Excel (.xlsx)” button at the top of this page or visit /api/enquiry/export-excel anytime to get the latest updated file immediately on any mobile phone, laptop, or tablet.
3
Instant Client Response:
Inside the table above (and inside the Excel sheet itself), you can click “💬 WhatsApp” to launch WhatsApp directly with the client's phone number and pre-filled message, or “📞 Call” to dial them instantly.
