Methodology & Workflow

How We Work Together

A simple, structured 5-step journey ensuring your ideas are transformed into polished, functional digital experiences with zero surprises.

01

Discovery & Scope Alignment

Every great project starts with listening. You submit our multi-step enquiry form outlining your vision, budget, and desired timeline. Founder Sangiv VP reviews your details, confirms project feasibility, and reaches out to clarify any details.

Outcome: A written project brief, confirmed timeline, and exact quotation milestone agreement before any payment or coding begins.
02

Wireframe & Visual Design

Next, we design the layout hierarchy, typography pairing, and component structure. For digital greetings, we craft the celebratory mood; for portfolios and businesses, we engineer high-converting sections that guide visitors smoothly.

Outcome: Visual layout approval from you before backend coding begins.
03

Clean Code Development

We write semantic, human-readable HTML5, performant CSS3, and modern Vanilla JavaScript without bloated libraries. Backend forms and APIs are implemented securely using Node.js and Express with automated email notifications.

Outcome: A functional working staging preview link where you can test the website on your own device.
04

Multi-Device Testing & Polish

Before launch, we run rigorous quality tests. We verify form validations, test responsive layouts across iOS and Android smartphones, audit load speeds, check color contrast accessibility, and ensure zero horizontal scrolling glitches.

Outcome: A fully vetted, bug-free website ready for public deployment.
05

Launch, Handover & Support

We deploy your website to its production destination (or supply shareable links for greeting projects). We hand over the complete clean codebase, explain how text and images can be updated, and remain available for launch support.

Outcome: Live online presence, full code ownership, and peace of mind.
