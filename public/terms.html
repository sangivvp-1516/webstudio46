2. Base Pricing & Milestone Quotations

All prices listed on our public service cards (Digital Greetings from ₹99, Portfolios from ₹350, Small Business Sites from ₹1,999, E-Commerce from ₹10,000) are indicative starting base rates.

  • Formal Scope of Work (SOW): The definitive project quotation is determined by specific architectural complexity, total custom page count, media requirements, external API integrations, and requested delivery speed.
  • Payment Structure: Unless mutually agreed in writing, standard custom developments follow a milestone schedule: a 50% advance booking deposit prior to project initiation, with the remaining 50% balance settlement due upon final staging review prior to live production deployment or source code release.

3. Client Deliverables & Content Responsibilities

Agreed project delivery timelines are contingent upon timely client cooperation. The Client agrees to:

  • Supply all necessary written copywriting, vector logos, and high-definition photography within the agreed onboarding window.
  • Warrant that all materials, media, and intellectual property supplied to WEB STUDIO 46 are owned by the Client or licensed with full legal authorization. The Client indemnifies WEB STUDIO 46 against any third-party copyright claims arising from client-provided media.
  • Provide centralized, consolidated feedback during designated design checkpoint reviews within 3 to 5 business days.

4. Revisions, Iterations & Scope Expansion

We pride ourselves on crafting digital experiences that exceed expectations. To maintain schedule integrity:

  • Included Revisions: Standard fixed-price packages include up to two (2) structured rounds of minor revisions (e.g. typography adjustments, color fine-tuning, spacing, or text modifications).
  • Out-of-Scope Modifications: Requests for net-new pages, complete structural wireframe pivots, or unquoted feature integrations made after design sign-off will be provided as an itemized Scope Amendment Addendum at standard studio hourly rates.

5. Intellectual Property & Code Ownership

100% Client Ownership Upon Final Settlement: Upon receipt of the final invoice payment in full, all proprietary rights, custom HTML5 markup, CSS3 styling, vanilla JavaScript logic, and bespoke design assets authored specifically for your commissioned project are transferred entirely to the Client.

Studio Showcase Right: WEB STUDIO 46 retains the non-exclusive professional right to reference the live project link, include screenshots in our digital portfolio, and cite the work in studio credentials as evidence of our craft.

6. Third-Party Services, Hosting & External Costs

Studio project fees cover bespoke design, frontend engineering, backend API development, and testing. Unless explicitly specified, project quotations do not include recurring third-party subscriptions:

  • Domain name registration and annual registrar renewals (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare, etc.).
  • Dedicated cloud hosting, virtual private servers (VPS), or managed application hosting.
  • Merchant payment gateway transaction processing percentages (Razorpay, Stripe, UPI providers).
  • Third-party transactional SMS, WhatsApp Business API credits, or paid commercial font licenses.

7. Browser Compatibility & Responsiveness Guarantee

All websites created by WEB STUDIO 46 are engineered to conform to modern W3C standards:

  • Supported Browsers: Optimized for the latest two major releases of Google Chrome, Apple Safari (iOS & macOS), Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge.
  • Responsive Viewports: Tested across standard mobile (360px+), tablet (768px+), laptop (1024px+), and high-resolution desktop (1440px+) breakpoints.
  • Legacy Browsers: Support for obsolete browsers (such as Internet Explorer) is explicitly deprecated.
