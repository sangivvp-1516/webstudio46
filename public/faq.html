Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers to help you understand our services, pricing structure, hosting guidance, and development workflow.

Every website project has distinct requirements. A single-page digital greeting with 3 photos requires far less time to build than an e-commerce website with 50 products, user accounts, and payment gateways. Our starting prices represent the base package. Once you submit your enquiry, founder Sangiv VP reviews your exact requirements and provides a transparent, fixed quotation.
For Digital Greeting Websites (₹99+), a free temporary shareable hosting link is included so you can share it immediately on WhatsApp. For Portfolios and Business Websites, domain registration (e.g., yourbusiness.com) and cloud hosting are purchased under your own name to ensure you retain 100% legal ownership. We assist you step-by-step with domain registration and server setup.
After you submit your initial enquiry through our form, we connect with you via email or WhatsApp. You can share your text, photos, logos, and reference links via Google Drive, WhatsApp, or direct email attachment.
Birthday and Anniversary greeting websites can typically be delivered within 24 to 48 hours once all photos and celebratory text are provided. If you have an urgent deadline for tomorrow, make sure to note that in Step 3 of the project enquiry form.
We craft frontends using semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript, paired with Node.js and Express for backend workflows. We deliberately avoid heavy frontend frameworks like React or Angular for standard sites because native web standards load faster, consume less mobile battery, eliminate unnecessary dependencies, and are easy for clients to maintain.
We develop responsive mobile web applications and progressive prototypes that work seamlessly inside mobile browsers on Android and iOS. Native mobile apps deployed to the App Store or Play Store require separate mobile SDKs and are quoted separately upon architectural review.
We accept UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), direct NEFT/IMPS bank transfers, and international bank transfers for overseas clients. Projects are structured with clear milestones agreed upon prior to project kickoff.
