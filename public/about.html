Guiding Values

Our Development Philosophy

The core principles that guide every line of code written at WEB STUDIO 46.

1. Core Standards Over Framework Bloat

We build primarily with semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript, paired with reliable Node.js backends. By relying on native web standards instead of massive framework dependencies, your website loads instantly, runs smoothly on any smartphone, and remains easy to edit and maintain for years to come.

2. Mobile-First & Accessible to Everyone

Most users will experience your brand from a smartphone screen. Every layout is planned mobile-first, ensuring high-contrast readability, generous touch target buttons (min 44px), zero horizontal overflow, and accessible screen-reader compatibility.

3. Transparent Pricing & Direct Communication

There are no account managers or middlemen here. You speak directly with founder Sangiv VP. We state our starting prices openly in Indian Rupees (₹), specify exactly what is included in each package, and provide honest advice rather than trying to upsell unnecessary features.

4. Genuine Craft Over Fabricated Claims

We believe trust is earned through authentic work, not exaggerated claims. We never invent fake customer testimonials, awards, or multi-year corporate histories. We let our working prototypes, clean code, and attentive service speak for themselves.
